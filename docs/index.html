How to read this

Buy at is the live Polymarket US price for that side; the American odds include Polymarket's taker fee. Fair chance blends the de-vigged sportsbook consensus (70%) with an independent stats model (30%). A bet only appears when both say it's underpriced. Exp. profit is the average result per $10 if you made this bet many times — it is not a guarantee. Risk rises for long shots, props, alt lines far from the sportsbook line, thin markets, injuries, wind/rain, and model disagreement. Parlay payouts are set in the Polymarket app when you build the combo, so each parlay lists the minimum payout that still makes it worth it. Nothing here stays open past Monday Night Football.

Paper bankroll started at $100 and stakes are quarter-Kelly, capped at 3% per bet. This is a research tool, not financial advice.
